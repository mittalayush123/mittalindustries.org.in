The beginning

One founder.
One conviction.
A lasting legacy.

Established in 1994, Mittal Industries began with a vision shaped by determination and an unwavering belief in long-term business relationships. Founded and single-handedly built by Mr. Surendra Mittal, the company grew from the ground up through perseverance, discipline and uncompromising business ethics.

The early years demanded resilience. In a competitive industry, every relationship, every consignment and every decision had to earn trust. Through difficult markets and changing conditions, Mr. Mittal continued to build credibility one commitment at a time.

That discipline remains part of the company today. Mittal Industries has evolved into a professionally managed organization serving industrial customers through dependable sourcing, authorized distribution partnerships and a reputation built over decades.

What began as one entrepreneur's determination to build something of lasting value became a business whose reputation was earned through consistency—not promises.

The journey

Progress without losing the principles that built us.

As the company grew, its role became larger than simply supplying materials. Mittal Industries developed deep familiarity with the technical expectations of the diamond tools and industrial materials sector, where consistency and reliability are essential.

Through authorized distribution relationships and close engagement with manufacturers and customers, the company has built a portfolio around trusted materials and dependable service. Our position today has been created through sustained performance rather than short-term promises.

Mr. Mittal's involvement in the wider industry has also included participation in professional bodies and associations connected with the diamond tools sector. The same commitment to the industry continues to guide the company as it moves into its next chapter.
