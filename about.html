Legacy. Leadership. Excellence.

For more than three decades, Mittal Industries has built its reputation through reliability, disciplined business values and a deep understanding of India’s diamond tools and industrial raw materials sector.

Our Journey Since 1994

Established in 1994, Mittal Industries began with a vision driven by determination and an unwavering belief in long-term business relationships. Founded and single-handedly built by Mr. Surendra Mittal, the company was developed from the ground up through perseverance, discipline and uncompromising business ethics.

The early journey was not without challenges. In a competitive market, every relationship, every consignment and every decision had to earn trust. Through resilience and integrity, Mr. Mittal steadily transformed challenges into progress, building credibility and long-term partnerships across the industry.

Over the years, his deep involvement in the sector has also led to prominent industry participation, including associations connected with the Diamond Tools Association of India and FIGSI. Today, Mittal Industries stands among India’s respected suppliers of premium diamond tools raw materials and industrial materials.

What began as one entrepreneur’s commitment to quality and trust has evolved into a professionally managed organization built around reliable sourcing, ethical trade practices and long-term industrial partnerships.

Industry Leadership

Our position has been built over decades of sustained performance rather than short-term promises. Through authorized distribution partnerships and close relationships with manufacturers, we focus on technical reliability, consistent material quality and dependable supply.

Our longevity is the result of a simple principle: earn trust through performance, then protect that trust through consistency.
